Match colors, not rows.

Juicefall is a falling-jelly puzzle for iPhone. Line up three jellies of one color and they burst into juice, and whatever falls after can match again.

Free · No ads · No subscription · Plays offline

Soft jellies, juicy chains

Jelly shapes drop into a garden well. Slide and turn them, and line up three or more of one color in a row or a column. They burst, the jellies above fall, and a new match sets off the next. Every step of a cascade multiplies your points.

Full rows do nothing here. Color is what counts, so every drop is a small puzzle.

Fruit with a job

Bigger matches make fruit: a blueberry clears its row and a banana knocks jellies loose. Six more fruit do more, from an orange that clears its column to grapes that take a whole color.

Three ways to play

Classic speeds up every level. Calm has no gravity: shapes fall only when you drop them. Hectic levels up twice as fast.

A puzzle a day

The daily challenge is the same puzzle for everyone, every day, with its own record.

Made for iPhone

120 Hz on ProMotion iPhones, Game Center leaderboards and 27 achievements, and your records kept in iCloud.

Made for everyone

Color symbols, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver support. In English, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish.

Yours, and private

No account and no tracking: the game collects no data. It plays offline, on a plane or underground.

No ads. No subscription. Yours to keep.

Every mode is free. After your first game, your next five have every fruit, so you can try them all. Each fruit is then a one-time purchase, all six together cost less, and Family Sharing is included.

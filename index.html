Juicefall

A falling-jelly puzzle for iPhone where colors matter, not rows.

Jelly shapes drop into a garden well. Line up three or more jellies of one color in a row or column and they burst, and whatever was above falls and can match again. Full rows do nothing.

  • Swap two jellies to finish a match, and chain cascades for bigger scores.
  • Make fruit with bigger matches: blueberries, oranges, strawberries, grapes and bananas, each with its own power.
  • New colors join as you level up.
  • Three ways to play: Classic, Calm and Hectic.
  • No ads, no account, and it plays offline.

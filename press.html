In one line

Juicefall is a falling-jelly puzzle for iPhone where you match colors, not rows.

In a paragraph

Jelly shapes drop into a narrow well. Line up three or more jellies of one color, in a row or a column, and they burst into juice; whatever falls after can match again, and every step of a cascade multiplies the points. Full rows do nothing. Bigger matches make fruit with a job, from a blueberry that clears its row to grapes that take a whole color. Free to play, with ads between games and one purchase, the full game, that removes them. No subscription and no account.

About the game

In Juicefall color is what counts, so every drop is a small color puzzle. The jellies are soft bodies that wobble, squish and burst, and a match makes the jellies above fall into new matches, so one good drop can set off a chain whose every step multiplies the points.

Landings and clears fill a swap meter that banks up to three swaps. When a shape lands, glowing jellies show every swap that makes a match, so a near miss becomes the start of the next chain.

Bigger matches make fruit. Four in a row make a blueberry that clears its row, four in a column an orange that clears its column, an L or T shape a strawberry that bursts, and five in a line grapes that take a whole color. Two different fruit swapped together fuse into a kiwi with both powers, twice as strong, and two of a kind into a super fruit, three times as strong.

There are three ways to play. Classic speeds up every level. Calm has no gravity: shapes fall only when you drop them. Hectic levels up twice as fast. A daily challenge gives everyone the same puzzle each day.

Every mode, the daily challenge and the lessons are free, with ads between games. One purchase, the full game, removes the ads and adds six fruit, with Family Sharing. There is no subscription, and it plays offline.

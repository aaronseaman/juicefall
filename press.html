In one line

Juicefall is a falling-jelly puzzle for iPhone where you match colors, not rows.

In a paragraph

Jelly shapes drop into a narrow well. Line up three or more jellies of one color, in a row or a column, and they burst into juice; whatever falls after can match again, and every step of a cascade multiplies the points. Full rows do nothing. Some matches make fruit with a job, from a blueberry that clears its row to grapes that take a whole color. Free to play, with ads between games and one purchase, the full game, that removes them and adds six fruit. No subscription and no account.

About the game

In Juicefall color is what counts, so every drop is a small color puzzle. The jellies are soft bodies that wobble, squish and burst, and a match makes the jellies above fall into new matches, so one good drop can set off a chain whose every step multiplies the points.

Landings and clears fill a swap meter that banks up to three swaps. When a shape lands, glowing jellies show every swap that makes a match, so a near miss becomes the start of the next chain.

Some matches make fruit. A blueberry clears its row and a banana knocks jellies off the board; the full game adds an orange that clears its column, a strawberry that bursts, grapes that take a whole color, a pineapple, a dragon fruit and the kiwi. Two fruit of a kind swapped together fuse into a super fruit, three times as strong; with the full game, two different fruit fuse into a kiwi with both powers.

There are three ways to play. Wild speeds up every level. Calm has no gravity: shapes fall only when you drop them. Hectic levels up twice as fast, with freeze diamonds: when a fruit sets one off, the jellies round it freeze for a few seconds. Quirky Play is a 90-second game with a new quirk each day, the same for everyone.

Every mode, Quirky Play and the tutorials are free, with ads between games. One purchase, the full game, removes the ads and adds six fruit, with Family Sharing. There is no subscription, and it plays offline.

Features

  • Match colors, not rows: three or more of one color in a row or a column burst; full rows do nothing
  • Cascades that multiply the points at every step
  • A swap meter that banks up to three swaps, with glowing hints for every swap that makes a match
  • Fruit made by the shape of a match, and fusions: two of a kind make a super fruit; with the full game, two different fruit make a kiwi
  • Wild, Calm and Hectic, and Quirky Play, a new quirk each day
  • Soft-body jellies; light and dark appearance, each with its own palette
  • A Home Screen widget in two sizes; Siri and Shortcuts (“Play Juicefall”, “Open Quirky Play in Juicefall”)
  • Game Center leaderboards and 27 achievements; records kept in iCloud
  • Every color a shape of its own; VoiceOver actions; six languages
  • One purchase for the full game, no subscription, no account; plays offline

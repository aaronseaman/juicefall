Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at philippinepogi@icloud.com and we'll answer you. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

Email support

Or send a request on GitHub, where it is public and needs a free GitHub account; please don't include personal details there.

How do I play?

On the menu, tap How to play. Drag to move a shape, tap to turn it, and line up three or more jellies of one color in a row or column to clear them. Full rows do nothing. When you have a swap banked, tap a glowing jelly and then a neighbor to finish a match.

When do new colors come?

Every game starts with red, orange and blue. Green joins at level 4, purple at 6, yellow at 9 and pink at 11.

What do the fruit do?

Each fruit has its own power, from clearing a row or column to bursting an area or taking a whole color. In the game's store, tap a fruit's picture to see what it does.

What does the full game add?

It removes every ad and adds six fruit to every game: the orange, strawberry, grapes, pineapple, dragon fruit and kiwi. Each has a free one-minute lesson (How to play, then "Try each fruit in a lesson"), bought or not.

I bought the full game but still see ads or no fruit

Tap Restore purchases at the foot of the store, or in Settings (the cog on the menu), signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. The pineapple comes now and then in your shapes from the start of a game, and the dragon fruit once pink has joined.

Report an ad

Ads come from Google AdMob and are limited to content for general audiences. If one should not have been shown, tell us: in the game, open Settings (the cog on the menu), then Report an ad, which starts an email to us; or email us with the subject "Report an ad". What did the ad show, and about when did you see it? A screenshot helps. We raise it with Google.

Where the law requires your consent to ads, Settings › Privacy choices opens the consent form again.

Why is the price different from what a friend sees?

The App Store sets each price in your country's currency, so it can differ from place to place.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My scores are missing on a new iPhone

High scores follow you through iCloud when iCloud is turned on for the game. Leaderboards in Game Center follow your Game Center account.

Privacy

Juicefall has no accounts and no analytics. Without the full game, its ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show them. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.

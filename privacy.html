Juicefall Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Juicefall for iPhone. Last updated 16 October 2026.

In short

Juicefall does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it plays without an internet connection.

On your iPhone

Your saved game, your high scores, your settings and which fruit you own are kept on your iPhone so the game can remember them. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for Juicefall, your high scores are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores them; we cannot see them.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in to it, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in your profile. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center. The game does not keep your Game Center details.

Purchases

Fruit are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never receive your name or payment details. The game only learns which fruit are owned.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You can delete the game's data by deleting the game, and turn iCloud and Game Center on or off in Settings on your iPhone.

Contacting us

If you ask for help through our support page, your request is posted on GitHub, where it is public and GitHub's privacy statement applies. Please do not include personal details there. We use what you write only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and on this page, with a new date.

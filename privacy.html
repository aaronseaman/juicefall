Privacy Policy

Juicefall for iPhone. Last updated 29 October 2026.

In short

Without the full game, Juicefall shows ads from Google. To show and measure them, and to see how the game performs and is used, Google's software in the game collects data about your device and how you use the game, and with your permission it tracks you across other companies' apps and websites (see Ads). The game itself plays without an internet connection.

On your iPhone

Your saved game, your high scores and progress, your settings and whether you own the full game are kept on your iPhone so the game can remember them. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it all.

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for Juicefall, your high scores, fruit mastery and Quirky Play records are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores them; we cannot see them.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in to it, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in your profile. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center. The game keeps Game Center's player ID for Juicefall on your iPhone, only to send your scores and achievements to the right player; it is never sent to us.

Ads

Without the full game, the game shows ads from Google AdMob between games and on the card after a game. To show and measure them, and to see how the game performs and is used, Google's software in the game collects data such as your device's model and settings, identifiers for this app and your device, a rough location from your internet address, performance and crash data, and how you use the game and the ads. Google's privacy policy covers it, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services explains more. The game asks once for your permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google may also use your device's advertising identifier to choose and measure ads; you can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ad is shown, and Settings › Privacy choices changes it. To report an ad, use Settings › Report an ad, or the support page. The full game removes the ads, and Google's software is then not started.

Purchases

The full game is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never receive your name or payment details. The game only learns whether the full game is owned.

Children

Juicefall is not made for children. Its ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

You can delete the data the game keeps on your iPhone by deleting the game, change your consent to ads in Settings › Privacy choices where it is offered, allow or stop tracking in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and turn iCloud and Game Center on or off in Settings on your iPhone. Google's privacy policy explains your choices for the data its software collects.

Contacting us

Email us at philippinepogi@icloud.com. If you email us for help, we use your message and your address only to answer you, and we don't share them. If you ask through GitHub instead (from our support page), your request is public and GitHub's privacy statement applies, so please don't include personal details there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and on this page, with a new date.
